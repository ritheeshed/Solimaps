Soli Maps

Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

What we collect

When you continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We save your name and email, the date you first and last visited, how many times you visited, the language you chose, and the link or campaign that brought you here. We do not get your Google password and we do not see anything else in your Google account.

Why

  • To run your Soli Maps account so you stay signed in.
  • To send you occasional Chennai job updates. Every email will have a way to stop them.
  • To understand, in total, how many people use Soli Maps and where they heard about it.

What we never do

We do not sell or rent your details, and we do not share them with employers or recruiters.

Where it is kept

Your details are kept in a private Google Sheet that only the Soli Maps team can open. Your profile picture is only shown in your own browser and is not saved by us. Your browser also remembers that you are signed in for up to 180 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your details: open the account menu (your picture at the top of the map) and choose Delete my details. Your row is removed from our sheet.
  • Sign out: use Sign out in the same menu.
  • You can also remove Soli Maps from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Your location

If you set where you start from, it is saved only in your own browser so the map can show distance and travel time. We never receive or store it. To find your area by name, your search text goes to Photon (komoot), an OpenStreetMap place search. To work out the road route, your starting point and the office are sent to the OSRM route service. You can remove your location any time with Remove my location on the map.

Jobs on the map

Openings come from company career pages and Indeed. When you tap Apply you go to that site, and its own privacy policy applies there.

Soli Maps is an independent project made in Chennai.
